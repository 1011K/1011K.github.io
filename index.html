Questions an analyst must answer

  1. 01Which segment assumptions drive consolidated value?
  2. 02Are capital intensity and reinvestment appropriately modelled?
  3. 03Are segment scopes, liabilities and cash flows reconciled?
  4. 04What would have to change for the investment thesis to be wrong?

A useful conclusion must survive changes in growth, reinvestment and the cost of capital. A model is a framework for testing those assumptions, not a substitute for judgement.

Valuation is not a single number.

FCFF estimates the value of cash flows available to capital providers. The assumptions behind those cash flows determine what the estimate means.

SOTP separates different economics.

Conglomerate segments may need different operating drivers and valuation frameworks before they can be reconciled into one view.

Risk belongs beside return.

Portfolio weights, covariance, drawdowns and concentration help explain how an attractive isolated idea can change a portfolio's overall risk.
