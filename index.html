Independent project · 2026 – present · In development

ASTRA, an equity and market research platform

ASTRA brings together fundamental research, technical analysis, company developments, derivatives and quant, portfolio intelligence and screening, so that evidence from different lenses can be read side by side with its assumptions and limitations visible. It is not an offer of investment advice or a trading signal service.
