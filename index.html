04Five research cases

Five companies.
Five business models.

Each case routes to the valuation method its economics allow. All five are under verification; reports and workbooks are published only after individual owner approval.

How a valuation is built here

  1. Choose the method from the business model. Banks and NBFCs use residual income, developers use project net asset value, telecom uses free cash flow to the firm. A method that does not fit the economics is rejected, not forced.
  2. Start from reported statements. Annual figures are taken from filings, with scope (consolidated or standalone), units and fiscal period stated, and corporate actions such as splits and bonus issues adjusted for.
  3. Forecast the drivers, not the output. Subscribers and ARPU, loan growth and credit cost, launches and collections: the assumptions are explicit and every one is editable.
  4. Cost of capital with stated sources. Risk-free rate, beta and equity risk premium are listed with their basis, and the terminal growth rate must stay below the discount rate.
  5. Reconcile, then stress. Accounting identities are checked, the enterprise-to-equity bridge is shown line by line, and the result is tested against changes in the discount rate and terminal growth.
  6. State the limits. Every case lists what is not measured, what would change the view, and who prepared it. No rating or price target is published.
